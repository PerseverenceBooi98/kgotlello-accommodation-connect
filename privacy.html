Privacy Policy

How Kgotlello Accommodation Connect collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kgotlello Accommodation Connect ("Kgotlello", "we", "our" or "us") handles personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect information including:

  • Names and contact details such as telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Accommodation preferences and rental requirements.
  • Property addresses or location descriptions, rental prices and facilities.
  • Property photographs and information submitted by landlords.
  • Messages, enquiries and communication records.
  • Referral and placement records associated with the platform.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

2. How we use information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Display approved accommodation listings.
  • Respond to tenant and landlord enquiries.
  • Connect prospective tenants with relevant landlords.
  • Review and manage property submissions.
  • Track enquiries and referrals originating from the platform.
  • Communicate about the status of submissions and enquiries.
  • Maintain, protect and improve the website.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing of information

Kgotlello may share relevant information where reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

  • Tenant contact details may be shared with a relevant landlord when the tenant makes an enquiry.
  • Landlord contact and property information may be made available to prospective tenants through approved listings.
  • Service providers may process information on our behalf to support website hosting, storage, security and operations.
  • Information may be disclosed where required or authorised by law.

We do not intend to sell personal information to unrelated third parties.

4. Property information and public listings

Landlords should only submit property information and photographs that they are authorised to share. Approved listing information may be visible to website visitors.

We aim to limit publicly displayed personal contact information and provide relevant contact details through the enquiry process where appropriate.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure.

Information may be stored using third-party hosting, database and storage providers. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention of information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including managing enquiries, property listings, referrals, disputes and legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified where appropriate and legally permissible.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or destruction of information where legally appropriate.
  • Object to or withdraw consent for certain processing activities.
  • Raise a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

8. Cookies and website technology

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for functionality, security or performance. Any optional tracking technologies will be addressed through appropriate consent mechanisms where required.

9. Third-party services

The website may rely on external service providers for hosting, databases, file storage and other technical services. Those providers may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy arrangements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform develops or where legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact and privacy enquiries

If you have a question, request or complaint relating to your personal information, please use our Contact Us page.

Our official privacy contact details will be published once confirmed.

Important: This is an initial website privacy policy draft. It must be reviewed and completed with the responsible party's legal identity, privacy contact details, retention periods, service-provider arrangements and applicable POPIA procedures before the platform begins collecting real personal information.
