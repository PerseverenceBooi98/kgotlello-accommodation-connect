About Kgotlello

Connecting people with places to call home.

Kgotlello Accommodation Connect is a local accommodation platform focused on making it easier for people to find rental opportunities in Mahikeng and nearby communities.

Why we exist

Making local accommodation easier to find.

Finding a suitable place to rent can take time. Information is often scattered across different channels, and prospective tenants may struggle to compare available options.

Kgotlello aims to bring those opportunities together in one accessible place. Tenants can explore properties according to location, rental price and facilities, while landlords have a straightforward way to present their available accommodation.

Our focus starts with Mahikeng and its surrounding communities, with an emphasis on useful information, clear communication and responsible connections.
