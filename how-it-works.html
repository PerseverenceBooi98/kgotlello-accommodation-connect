Referral arrangement

A successful connection matters.

The proposed landlord referral fee is between R200 and R300 when a tenant sourced through Kgotlello successfully secures the property. The exact amount and payment terms will be agreed with the landlord.

A few things to remember

Stay informed and make confident decisions.

Check before paying

Always view the property, confirm the landlord's details and understand the rental agreement before making any payment.

Listings are reviewed

Submitted properties are subject to review before publication. A listing should not be treated as a guarantee of availability.

Confirm the details

Rental prices, deposits, utilities, viewing arrangements and availability should be confirmed directly before entering an agreement.
