These Terms and Conditions govern access to and use of the Kgotlello Accommodation Connect website and its accommodation connection services.

1. About the platform

Kgotlello Accommodation Connect is a locally focused accommodation listing and referral platform intended to connect prospective tenants with landlords offering rental accommodation in Mahikeng and nearby areas.

The platform provides listing, discovery and enquiry facilities. Unless separately agreed in writing, Kgotlello is not the landlord, property owner, estate agent or party to a rental agreement.

By using the platform, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and truthful information.
  • Use the platform for legitimate accommodation-related purposes.
  • Respect the privacy and rights of other users.
  • Not submit misleading, fraudulent or unlawful content.
  • Not attempt to interfere with the security or operation of the website.

3. Property listings

Landlords may submit accommodation details for consideration. Submission does not guarantee approval or publication.

Kgotlello may review, reject, suspend, amend or remove a listing where information is incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, unsuitable or otherwise inconsistent with platform requirements.

Landlords are responsible for ensuring that they have the authority to advertise the property and that submitted photographs and information may lawfully be used.

4. Accuracy and availability

Property details, photographs, rental prices, facilities and availability are supplied by landlords or their authorised representatives.

Although listings may be reviewed, Kgotlello cannot guarantee that every detail is complete, accurate or current. Prospective tenants must independently confirm all relevant information with the landlord.

5. Tenant responsibilities

Prospective tenants should:

  • Confirm the property's location and availability.
  • Arrange an appropriate viewing before committing.
  • Verify the identity and authority of the landlord.
  • Confirm rental amounts, deposits, utilities and other charges.
  • Review the rental agreement before signing.
  • Avoid making payments until satisfied that the arrangement is legitimate.

6. Landlord responsibilities

Landlords using the platform agree to:

  • Provide truthful property and contact information.
  • Have the legal authority to advertise the property.
  • Ensure that photographs and descriptions accurately represent the accommodation.
  • Notify Kgotlello when a listed property is no longer available.
  • Respond to enquiries honestly and professionally.
  • Comply with applicable rental, consumer protection and other relevant laws.

7. Enquiries and referrals

Enquiries submitted through Kgotlello may be recorded and associated with the relevant property and landlord to help manage communication and identify referrals originating from the platform.

Users should provide accurate contact details and communicate honestly about their interest in a property.

8. Proposed landlord referral fee

Kgotlello intends to operate a referral arrangement under which a landlord may be required to pay an agreed fee when a tenant introduced through the platform successfully secures the property.

The proposed fee is between R200 and R300 per successful placement. The exact amount, what constitutes a successful referral, payment deadline, proof of referral and any dispute process must be agreed with the landlord before the arrangement becomes binding.

Submitting a property for consideration does not, by itself, create an obligation to pay this referral fee.

9. No guarantee of rental placement
